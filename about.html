About the firm

Independent advice, built around your business.

Scalehaven is an independent strategy and management consulting firm for leadership teams of growing and mid-sized companies.

Our story

Built for companies on the way up

Growing companies face the same strategic questions as large corporations — but rarely get the same quality of advice.

Large consultancies are built for large clients: big teams, long timelines and fees to match. Smaller advisors are often too narrow, or too close to a particular software vendor or solution. Leadership teams in between are left to make high-stakes decisions without an independent, rigorous partner.

Scalehaven was founded to close that gap. Our name reflects what we stand for: helping companies scale — and being a safe haven for honest, independent advice along the way.

We are deliberately selective about the work we take on. Every engagement is led by senior consultants who stay involved from the first conversation to the final result, so each client receives our full attention.

What we believe

The principles behind every engagement

01

Candor over comfort

We tell you what the evidence says, even when it is not what anyone hoped to hear. Honest advice is the most valuable thing we offer.

02

Pragmatism over theory

Recommendations are only useful if your organization can execute them. We design for the company you are, not an idealized one.

03

Confidential by default

Your data, plans and people are handled with discretion. Mutual NDAs are standard on request, and we never share client information.

04

Measurable outcomes

We define success up front, agree on how to measure it, and report against it throughout the engagement.
