FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kind of companies do you work with?

We focus on growing and mid-sized companies — typically privately held, family-owned or investor-backed businesses, as well as business units of larger corporations. If your situation is different, we are still happy to talk.

How does an engagement start?

With a free, no-obligation consultation of 30–45 minutes. If there is a fit, we send a written proposal with scope, deliverables, timeline and fees, usually within a few business days.

How are your fees structured?

Most projects are quoted as a fixed fee for a clearly defined scope, so there are no surprises. Advisory work is billed as a monthly retainer. We will recommend the model that best fits your situation.

Do you work remotely or on site?

Both. We combine efficient remote collaboration with on-site workshops and leadership sessions wherever being in the room makes a difference.

How do you handle confidential information?

Confidentiality is standard in every engagement. We are happy to sign a mutual NDA before any sensitive information is shared, and we use client information only for the purposes of the engagement.

Ready to talk about your next big decision?

Book a free, no-obligation consultation. We will listen, ask the right questions and tell you honestly whether — and how — we can help.
