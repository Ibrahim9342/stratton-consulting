How we work

A disciplined path from insight to impact.

Every engagement follows the same four phases, scaled to the size of your question. You always know where we are, what comes next and what it will deliver.

The four phases

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Weeks 1–3

    We build a shared, fact-based understanding of where you stand and what really drives performance.

    Typical deliverables

    • Leadership and stakeholder interviews
    • Analysis of financial, market and operational data
    • Hypotheses tested against evidence
    • Fact base and key issues summary
  2. 02

    Design

    Weeks 3–6

    We develop and evaluate options with your team, quantify the trade-offs and agree on a clear direction.

    Typical deliverables

    • Strategic options with quantified impact
    • Recommended direction and rationale
    • Roadmap with initiatives, owners and milestones
    • Business case and success metrics
  3. 03

    Deliver

    Months 2–6+

    We work alongside your team to turn the plan into results — with governance, pace and accountability.

    Typical deliverables

    • Program governance and PMO
    • Hands-on support for priority initiatives
    • Regular progress and risk reporting
    • Course corrections based on results
  4. 04

    Scale

    Ongoing

    We make sure improvements stick and keep growing after our involvement ends.

    Typical deliverables

    • Results measured against the business case
    • Playbooks, tools and templates handed over
    • Coaching for internal owners
    • Optional advisory check-ins

Getting started

Your first 30 days with us

No lengthy sales process. Within a month of our first conversation, you have a clear plan and first insights on the table.

  1. Day 1

    Introductory call

    A 30–45 minute conversation about your situation, goals and constraints.

  2. Week 1

    Written proposal

    Scope, deliverables, team, timeline and a fixed fee — in writing, with no obligation.

  3. Week 2

    Kick-off

    We align on objectives, success metrics, stakeholders and the data we need.

  4. Week 4

    First insights

    A structured fact base and initial findings, discussed openly with your leadership team.
